Site settings.

- mood is used when your newest post doesn't have its own "mood:" line.
- playlist: a Spotify playlist, album, or track link. It shows up as the music
  player at the top of your profile. Leave it empty to hide it.
- song: put an audio file in content/media and write its file name here
  (like "song: my track.mp3"), plus a song_title. It never autoplays.
- details: one per line, as "Label | Value".
- background_effect: "pixels" for little square specks slowly drifting up,
  "grain" for moving film grain with dust and scratches, or "none" for a
  still background.
- background_image: a file in content/media (like a scan of a print) tiles
  behind the page, under the effect. background_dots adds little dots instead.
- films_to_show: how many recent Letterboxd films to list under Movies.
  0 turns the list off.
