This recipe came from the parents of Paiton’s hawaiian college tennis teammate.

## Ingredients

*For 3lbs meat*

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup soy sauce
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 2 cloves garlic, grated
- 1/4 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 1 Tbsp sesame seeds
- 1/4 cup chopped green onions
