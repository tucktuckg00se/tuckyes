Great on chicken, potatoes, steak, sandwiches. I haven't found anything it's bad on yet, just things it's good on and things it's really good on.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup mayonnaise or 50/50 with Greek yogurt
- 1 cup tightly packed fresh cilantro leaves and stems
- Small handful of mint leaves
- 2 green onions, root end removed
- 1 whole jalapeño pepper
- 2 garlic cloves
- ¼ cup parmesan
- Juice of 1 lime, about 2 tablespoons
- Coarse salt to taste

## Notes

- If you can find them, replace the mint leaves with huacatay and the jalapeño with aji amarillo
- I prefer thicker sauce, so sometimes I end up using up to twice the amount of cilantro and parmesan.
