## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
- 1 Tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne
- 4 cups (12oz) pecan halves, unsalted
- 1 egg white, whisked

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 300 degrees F
1. Add the sugars, cinnamon, salt & cayenne to a large bowl. Whisk until combined.
1. In a separate bowl add pecans & egg white, toss. Add sugar mixture, toss until combined.
1. Spread mixture in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake for 40 minutes, giving them a stir every 20 minutes. Remove from the oven and let cool.
