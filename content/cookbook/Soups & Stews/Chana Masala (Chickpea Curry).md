## Ingredients

- 2 cups dry chickpeas (or two cans)
- Salt
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 medium (~220-260 g) yellow onion, finely chopped
- 5 (~1 tbsp) garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp ginger, minced
- 1 Serrano pepper, minced
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp paprika powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1/2 tsp curry powder
- 1/4 tsp red chili powder
- Salt & pepper
- 2-3 tomatoes, finely chopped
- Cooked chickpeas
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 2 Tbsp lemon juice
- Cilantro leaves

## Steps

1. Add 2 cups of dry chickpeas to the instant pot. Skip to step 4 if using canned chickpeas.
1. Add water 1/2 to 1in. above chickpeas. Add some salt.
1. Pressure cook high 40-45 min.
1. Heat oil in a medium pot. Add the cumin seeds and onion. Sauté for 7-8 minutes, until the edges just start to brown.
1. Add the garlic, ginger, and green chili pepper and sauté for another 2-3 minutes.
1. Deglaze the pan with a tablespoon of water, and add all the powdered spices. Stir to roast the spices.
1. Add the tomatoes, salt and pepper. Continue to sauté for another 2-3 minutes.
1. Mix in the strained chickpeas, water and chicken stock.
1. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, stirring often, until reduced sufficiently.
1. Use a spatula to slightly crush the chickpeas.
1. Turn off the heat and add the garam masala, lemon juice, and cilantro leaves.
