## Ingredients

- 4 Roma tomatoes chopped (canned tomatoes are good, too, just dont use all the liquid from a big can)
- 2 red bell peppers chopped (other peppers a great here)
- 1-2 Scotch bonnet peppers chopped (to your heat preference – other hot peppers like haberno can be used, 2 of those is almost too hot for me)
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil coconut oil is great, too
- 1 medium onion chopped
- 4 cloves garlic chopped
- 4 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2-1/2 cups chicken stock
- 2 teaspoons curry powder or more to taste - optional, for extra flavor
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 2 cups white rice rinsed
- 2 bay leaves
- 1Tbsp Ginger
- Maybe some fermented honey garlic
- 1 chicken breast

## Steps

1. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers and scotch bonnet peppers together in a blender or food processor until smooth. Set aside.
1. Heat the oil in a large pot to medium-high heat and cook the chicken so it is brown on both sides but not fully cooked through. Remove from the oil and cut in to 1/2in pieces.
1. Add the onion to the remaining oil. Cook for 5 minutes to soften.
1. Add the garlic, ginger and tomato paste and stir. Cook 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
1. Add the tomato-pepper puree and stir. Cook for 1 minute, stirring, then reduce the heat to medium and cook for 15 minutes to let the flavors develop. The sauce will reduce and deepen in color.
1. Add chicken stock, curry powder, thyme and salt and pepper to taste. Bring to a boil for 5 minutes.
1. Add rice, chicken pieces and bay leaves and mix well. Reduce heat and cover. Simmer on low until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is cooked to your liking, about 20 minutes. You may need a bit longer. Add in water or chicken stock if needed for more moisture. It should be light and fluffy (unless you prefer wetter rice).
1. Remove from heat and remove the lid. Let sit for 5 minutes and fluff the rice with a fork or wooden spoon, as desired.
1. Serve!
