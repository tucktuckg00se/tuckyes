## Ingredients

### Dipping sauce

- 1 tablespoon soy sauce (good soy sauce)
- 2 teaspoons rice vinegar
- 1 teaspoon hot pepper flakes, optional
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
- Sesame oil
- Garlic
- Black Pepper
- Taste the sauce, make sure it isn't shitty.

### Haemul Pajeon (seafood green onion pancake)

- 4 ounces deveined and cleaned shrimp, chopped
- 4 ounces clam meat, chopped
- 8 ounces cleaned squid, chopped
- 1 green chili pepper, chopped
- 1-2 bundles green onions, chopped
- 1 tablespoon of chopped carrot
- ½ cup flour
- ½ cup potato starch (or rice flour)
- 1 egg
- ½ cup water
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons sesame oil
- 4 to 6 tablespoons peanut oil

## Steps

1. Cut seafood small so pancakes can flatten and cook correctly
1. Put the shrimp, clam, and squid into a bowl. Add green chili pepper, green onion, carrot, flour, potato starch, egg, water, fish sauce, salt, black pepper, and sesame oil. Mix it all together with a spoon.
1. Put oil in a pan and cook one side of the pancake until browned. Flip and press down the pancake with a spatula, cook until browned.
