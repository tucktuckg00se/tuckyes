I learned while writing in this dish, which I always associated with Christmas morning, that everyone else knows it as “funeral potatoes.”

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds of frozen hash browns
- 1 10.5oz can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 chopped onion (if desired)
- 2 cups shredded cheese
- 1 pound cooked bulk sausage (or bacon)

## Steps

1. Mix all together in a large bowl.
1. Pour into a 9x13 pan.
1. Top with 2 cups crushed corn flakes.
1. Bake at 350°F for 1.5 hours.
1. Cover with foil while baking.
