## Ingredients

- Taco sized tortillas
- Kimchi
- Skirt steak/carne asada

### Marinade (use blender)(marinate up to 24hrs)

- 1/2 cup shoyu (can substitute low-sodium soy sauce or tamari)
- 1/2 cup lightly packed dark brown sugar (can substitute light brown sugar)
- 2 tablespoons mirin or rice wine of choice
- 2 tablespoons toasted sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon gochujang
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 small Asian pear, peeled, cored, and roughly chopped (can substitute Pink Lady apple or Bosc pear)
- 1 small yellow onion, peeled and roughly chopped
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and roughly chopped
- kosher salt and ground black pepper, to season

### Avocado salsa

- 2 medium avocados, (diced)
- 1/2 medium red onion, (chopped fine)
- 2 limes, (squeezed)
- 1-2 tbsp freshly chopped cilantro, (to your taste preference)
- 1 scallion, (green and white, chopped)

## Steps

1. Blend ingredients for marinade and marinate meat for 24 hours.
