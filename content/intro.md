I live in the Wenatchee Valley. For about ten years I worked at NCWLIFE, the little TV station here. I started out behind a camera and ended up being the one who kept the whole thing on the air.

These days I take houses apart for a reuse store in Cashmere, so the lumber and fixtures get a second life. In between, I make things: software, music, prints, parts on the 3D printer, and whatever else I get curious about.
